# User guide

Budget Tracker keeps everything in your own browser. There is nothing to sign up for.

## First steps

1. Open the app. The **Dashboard** is empty and offers to add your first transaction.
2. Go to **Transactions**, choose **Expense** or **Income**, enter the amount (a dot or a comma both work), pick a category and a date, and press **Add transaction**.
3. Go back to the **Dashboard**: the month summary and the spending chart now show your data.

## Pages

| Page | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | See income, expenses and balance for the chosen month, warnings about limits, spending by category and your latest transactions. Use ‹ › to change the month. |
| **Transactions** | Add a transaction, or press **Edit** / **Delete** in the history. Filter by type, category, text, or the selected month. Deleting asks for confirmation. |
| **Reports** | Charts for expenses and income by category and a 6-month comparison. |
| **Limits** | Choose an expense category and a monthly limit. The bar turns orange at 80% and red when you pass the limit. Saving a limit for the same category replaces the old one. |
| **Settings** | Switch light/dark theme, choose the currency symbol, or delete all data. |

## Good to know

- Data is stored per browser and per device. Clearing site data in the browser removes it, and it does not move to another device.
- If the browser says storage is full or disabled, the app keeps working but cannot save. A red message tells you when this happens, and changes are lost when the tab is closed.
- If saved data is ever damaged, the app starts empty and shows a warning. A copy of the damaged data is kept in the browser under `budget-tracker.data.v1.corrupt`.
- Amounts can have at most 2 decimal places, dates must be between the year 2000 and one year from today.

## Accessibility

- Everything works with the keyboard (Tab, Enter, Space). A "Skip to content" link appears on the first Tab.
- Charts have text alternatives; colours are never the only signal (amounts also have + and − signs, limits have text).
- The layout adapts to phones, tablets and desktops, and the dark theme reduces glare.
